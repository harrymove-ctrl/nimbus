RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

Sample document for the Nimbus lease review template. All names, addresses and figures are invented.

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made on October 20, 2026 between Harborline Residential Holdings LLC ("Landlord"), whose office is at 900 Quay Street, Suite 410, Riverton, and Jordan Ellis ("Tenant"). Landlord and Tenant agree as follows.

1. PREMISES

1.1 Landlord leases to Tenant the apartment known as 214 Alder Street, Unit 3B, Riverton (the "Premises"), together with one assigned parking space (No. 17) and one storage locker in the basement (No. 3B).

1.2 The Premises are leased unfurnished, except for the refrigerator, range, dishwasher, washer and dryer listed in the Move-In Condition Report (Schedule A).

1.3 Tenant has inspected the Premises and accepts them in their present "as is" condition, except for items noted in writing on Schedule A within five (5) days after the Commencement Date.

2. TERM

2.1 The term of this Lease begins on December 1, 2026 (the "Commencement Date") and ends at 11:59 pm on November 30, 2027 (the "Initial Term"), unless ended earlier or renewed under this Lease.

2.2 If Landlord cannot deliver possession on the Commencement Date for any reason, Landlord is not liable to Tenant for any loss, and rent is abated only for the days possession is not delivered. Tenant may not cancel this Lease unless possession is delayed by more than sixty (60) days.

3. RENT

3.1 Monthly rent is $1,950.00, payable in advance on or before the first (1st) day of each month through Landlord's online portal or by certified check. Rent for any partial month is prorated by the day.

3.2 Rent is due without demand, deduction or set-off. Tenant may not withhold rent for any reason, including a dispute about repairs.

3.3 If rent is not received in full by 5:00 pm on the third (3rd) day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late fee of ten percent (10%) of the monthly rent, plus a further $25.00 for each day the rent remains unpaid, without limit.

3.4 A returned payment fee of $75.00 applies to every dishonoured check or failed electronic payment. After two returned payments, Landlord may require all future rent to be paid by certified funds.

3.5 Payments are applied first to fees, charges, interest and damages, and only then to rent.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND FEES

4.1 On signing, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,900.00 (two months' rent).

4.2 Tenant shall also pay a non-refundable administrative fee of $450.00 and a non-refundable move-in fee of $250.00. These fees are earned by Landlord on signing and are not part of the security deposit.

4.3 Landlord may apply the security deposit to unpaid rent, fees, cleaning, repainting, carpet replacement and any other amount Tenant owes. Landlord will return the balance, with an itemized statement, within forty-five (45) days after Tenant has vacated and returned all keys.

4.4 The security deposit does not earn interest for Tenant.

5. UTILITIES AND SERVICES

5.1 Tenant pays for electricity, gas, internet and cable. Landlord bills Tenant monthly for water, sewer and trash at a share calculated by Landlord using a ratio of the Premises' floor area to the building, plus a billing service fee of $6.50 a month.

5.2 Landlord is not liable for any interruption of utilities or services, whatever the cause, and rent is not reduced during any interruption.

6. USE AND OCCUPANCY

6.1 The Premises shall be used only as a private residence for Tenant and the following occupants: none listed.

6.2 Guests may not stay more than seven (7) nights in any thirty (30) day period without Landlord's written consent.

6.3 Tenant shall not run any business from the Premises that involves clients or customers visiting.

6.4 Tenant shall comply with the Building Rules (Schedule B), which Landlord may change at any time by posting the change in the lobby or sending it by email.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

7.1 Tenant shall keep the Premises clean, safe and in good condition, and shall promptly tell Landlord in writing of any defect, leak or damage.

7.2 Tenant shall pay for every repair to the Premises, including appliances, plumbing fixtures, doors, windows and locks, up to $500.00 per repair, whatever the cause of the repair, unless the damage was caused by Landlord's willful misconduct.

7.3 Landlord shall maintain the roof, structure, common areas and building systems serving more than one unit. Landlord will make repairs within a reasonable time, and Landlord decides what is reasonable.

7.4 Tenant shall replace smoke alarm batteries, light bulbs and the HVAC filter at Tenant's cost.

7.5 Tenant shall not paint, install fixtures, change locks or make any alteration without Landlord's written consent. Any alteration made becomes Landlord's property when Tenant leaves.

8. LANDLORD'S ACCESS

8.1 Landlord and Landlord's agents may enter the Premises at any reasonable time to inspect, repair or show the Premises to prospective tenants, buyers or lenders.

8.2 Landlord will try to give Tenant notice before entering, but notice is not required. Landlord may enter without notice at any time Landlord believes an emergency exists.

8.3 During the last sixty (60) days of the term, Landlord may place a "For Rent" sign on the Premises and show the Premises on weekdays and Saturdays.

9. PETS AND SMOKING

9.1 No pets are allowed without Landlord's written consent. If consent is given, Tenant shall pay pet rent of $55.00 a month per pet and a non-refundable pet fee of $400.00 per pet.

9.2 Smoking or vaping of any substance is not allowed in the Premises, on balconies or within twenty-five (25) feet of any entrance. A cleaning charge of $750.00 applies to any breach.

10. INSURANCE

10.1 Tenant shall keep renter's insurance with personal liability coverage of at least $300,000 throughout the term, naming Landlord as an additional insured, and shall give Landlord proof of coverage before moving in and on every renewal.

10.2 Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's property or Tenant's liability.

11. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

11.1 Landlord, its members, managers, employees and agents shall not be liable to Tenant, any occupant or any guest for any injury, death, loss or damage to person or property, from any cause whatsoever, including theft, fire, water, mold, pests, snow, ice, the failure of any building system, or the acts or omissions of other tenants, and including any injury, loss or damage caused by the negligence of Landlord or its agents, or by Landlord's failure to make repairs.

11.2 Tenant shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Landlord, its members, managers, employees and agents from and against all claims, liabilities, damages, fines, costs and attorney's fees arising out of or in connection with Tenant's use of the Premises, the building or the common areas, whether or not the claim is caused in part by the negligence of Landlord or its agents.

11.3 Tenant's obligations under this Section 11 survive the end of this Lease.

12. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

12.1 Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises, including by short-term rental, without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord may withhold in its sole discretion.

12.2 Tenant shall pay a processing fee of $300.00 with any request for consent, whether or not consent is given.

13. EARLY TERMINATION

13.1 Tenant may end this Lease before the end of the term only by giving sixty (60) days' written notice and paying, with the notice, an early termination fee equal to three (3) months' rent, in addition to rent through the termination date.

13.2 The early termination fee is not a penalty. It is a fair estimate of Landlord's loss, and Landlord has no duty to re-let the Premises or to reduce the fee if the Premises are re-let.

14. DEFAULT

14.1 Tenant is in default if Tenant fails to pay any rent or charge when due, breaks any other term of this Lease or the Building Rules, or abandons the Premises.

14.2 On default, Landlord may end this Lease by written notice and recover possession as the law allows, and Tenant shall remain liable for all rent for the rest of the term, together with Landlord's costs of re-letting, including advertising, broker fees and repainting.

14.3 Landlord may report any unpaid amount to credit reporting agencies.

15. ATTORNEY'S FEES

15.1 If Landlord employs an attorney or brings any action to enforce this Lease or to recover possession, Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable attorney's fees and costs, whether or not a lawsuit is filed.

16. MOVE-OUT

16.1 Tenant shall return the Premises clean, freshly painted in Landlord's standard color by a painter Landlord approves, with carpets professionally cleaned, and with all keys, fobs and remotes. A lost fob costs $95.00 to replace.

16.2 Any property Tenant leaves behind after move-out is considered abandoned and may be kept, sold or thrown away by Landlord without notice or liability.

17. NOTICES

17.1 Notices to Landlord must be in writing and sent by certified mail to Landlord's office. Notices to Tenant may be delivered in person, left at the Premises, or sent to Tenant's email address on file, and are effective when sent.

18. GENERAL

18.1 This Lease, with Schedules A, B and C, is the entire agreement. No promise by any agent of Landlord binds Landlord unless written in this Lease.

18.2 If any part of this Lease is held invalid, the rest stays in force.

18.3 Landlord's failure to enforce any term is not a waiver of that term.

18.4 Tenant waives any right to a jury trial in any action relating to this Lease.

18.5 Time is of the essence for every obligation of Tenant.

19. HOLDING OVER

19.1 If Tenant stays in the Premises after this Lease ends without a renewal under Section 26, Tenant shall pay rent at two hundred percent (200%) of the last monthly rent for every month or part of a month Tenant stays, and is liable for all of Landlord's resulting losses.

20. PARKING AND STORAGE

20.1 Parking space No. 17 is for one registered passenger vehicle only. Landlord may tow, at Tenant's cost, any vehicle that is inoperable, unregistered or parked outside the assigned space.

20.2 The storage locker is used at Tenant's own risk. Landlord is not a bailee of anything stored in it.

21. LEAD PAINT, MOLD AND RADON DISCLOSURES

21.1 The building was built in 1984. Landlord has no knowledge of lead-based paint hazards in the Premises.

21.2 Tenant shall keep the Premises ventilated and dry, report any moisture or visible mold within twenty-four (24) hours, and is responsible for any mold damage resulting from Tenant's failure to do so.

22. QUIET ENJOYMENT, NOISE AND COMMON AREAS

22.1 Tenant shall not make or allow noise that disturbs other residents, and quiet hours are 10:00 pm to 7:00 am every day.

22.2 Bicycles, strollers, shoes and other items may not be left in hallways, stairwells or the lobby. Landlord may remove and dispose of any item left in a common area after twenty-four (24) hours without further notice.

22.3 The roof deck, gym and laundry room may be used only during posted hours. Landlord may close any amenity at any time, for any length of time, without reducing rent.

23. KEYS, LOCKS AND ALARMS

23.1 Landlord will provide two (2) keys to the Premises, one (1) mailbox key and two (2) building fobs. Additional fobs cost $95.00 each.

23.2 Tenant shall not add or change any lock. A lockout service call by Landlord's staff costs $85.00 during business hours and $175.00 at any other time.

23.3 The Premises have working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms on the Commencement Date. Tenant shall test them monthly and shall not disconnect or remove them.

24. RELOCATION

24.1 Landlord may, on thirty (30) days' written notice, require Tenant to move to another unit in the building or in any other building owned or managed by Landlord in Riverton of similar size, for any reason, including renovation or a sale of the building.

24.2 If Landlord relocates Tenant, Landlord will pay reasonable moving costs up to $500.00. Rent for the new unit will be the rent then charged for that unit, and all other terms of this Lease apply to the new unit.

24.3 If Tenant refuses to relocate, Tenant is in default under Section 14.

25. SALE OF THE BUILDING

25.1 Landlord may sell or transfer the building at any time. On transfer of the security deposit to the new owner, Landlord is released from all liability to Tenant, including for the return of the deposit.

25.2 This Lease is subordinate to every present and future mortgage on the building, and Tenant shall sign any document a lender reasonably asks for within five (5) days of the request, failing which Landlord may sign it on Tenant's behalf.

26. RENEWAL

26.1 This Lease shall automatically renew for successive terms of twelve (12) months each (each a "Renewal Term") on the same terms, unless Tenant gives Landlord written notice of non-renewal, by certified mail, received no fewer than ninety (90) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then current Renewal Term.

26.2 Monthly rent for each Renewal Term shall be the rent in effect at the end of the prior term, increased by the greater of six percent (6%) or the percentage Landlord determines in its sole discretion to reflect market conditions, which Landlord may notify to Tenant by email at any time before the Renewal Term begins.

26.3 A renewal fee of $200.00 is charged at the start of each Renewal Term.

26.4 Landlord may decline to renew by giving Tenant thirty (30) days' notice before the end of any term.
