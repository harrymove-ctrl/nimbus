Timeline strip: playhead and spawn connector

src/components/nimbus/timeline.tsx · src/app/globals.css · the strip that shows what each agent is doing, live

Time is the only easing: bars grow with the clock, the playhead follows it, and a spawn draws one dotted line down.

Figure 1 · three lanes, a spawn, a failure, the playheaddrawn at 1× (layout px)
1 · Spawn dotPops on the parent lane: 160 ms spring, overshoot 1.05.
2 · ConnectorDraws top to bottom: 220 ms linear, dotted 2 3.
3 · WaitingHatch drifts 7 px per 1.2 s, linear, forever.
4 · Axis rescaleWindow × 1.25 at 80 %; eases 300 ms in-out.
5 · FailedOutline flashes once, then ✕ fades in.
  1. 1The playhead is the clock. It sits at elapsed time with no easing; while running it follows the wall clock, when done it freezes at run.end.
  2. 2A spawn is one dot and one dotted line. The dot sits under the parent's segment (8 px); the line runs down to the child lane's first segment.
  3. 3Dot on the axis row (10 px) marks the playhead's time on the ruler.
  4. 4Hatched means waiting. The orchestrator waits on its children; the same hatch marks a question or an approval.
  5. 5↓ and ↻ on a segment's cap: the router chose a cheaper tier, or a fallback happened.
TriggerTime itself; agent.spawn, segment.wait, agent.error and route events add marks.
Propertiessegment width = time × pxPerSec · pxPerSec = (plot − 24) ÷ max(25, elapsed × 1.25) · connector scaleY · dot scale · hatch translate
