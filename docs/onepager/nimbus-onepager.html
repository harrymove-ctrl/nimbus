What it does

  • Asks firstEvery side effect is a card. An approved one can become a rule, and you can revoke it.
  • LearnsMemories and a rewritten skill file after every run, so the next one asks less.
  • Priced per stepThree Nemotron sizes, routed by job, with the bill on screen while it runs.
  • YoursYour database, your keys, the tools you list. Nothing runs that you did not allow.
  • Always onSchedules in the app, and an OpenAI-compatible endpoint for OpenClaw and Hermes.
  • Open sourceMIT. pnpm dev runs it with no keys at all, on a scripted mock model.

How it uses Nemotron on Nebius

nvidia/Nemotron-3-Ultra-550b-a55bplans the run, routes each hop and reviews the result (the Orchestrator).
nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12bdoes the work: the Researcher, the Analyst and the Writer.
nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-A3Bchecks the output and writes the memories (the Checker and the Learner).

Every call goes to api.tokenfactory.nebius.com. Numbers above are measured on the recorded take of Oct 29, 2026, two real runs of the Morning brief, and listed in docs/video/RELEASE.md.
